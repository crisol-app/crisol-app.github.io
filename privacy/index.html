Privacy Policy — Crisol App

Last updated 2026-10-14

1. What data we collect

Data stored locally on your device

All personal data you enter into Crisol (quests, diet logs, notes, water intake, workout records, body measurements, and app preferences) is stored only on your device using SQLite. It is never transmitted to our servers.

Data collected by third-party SDKs

Google AdMob (all users) Crisol is free and supported by advertising, shown to all users via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect:

  • Device advertising ID
  • IP address and coarse location
  • App usage signals (ad impressions, click events)

We pass requestNonPersonalizedAdsOnly: true to AdMob by default. To learn more, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Open Food Facts (diet tracker search) When you search for foods, the app queries the Open Food Facts public API. Queries include the search term you type. No account or personal identifiers are sent. See Open Food Facts Privacy Policy.

Sentry (crash reporting, all users) If the app crashes, or a screen fails to draw, a crash report is sent to Sentry so we can fix the problem. A report contains the technical error and stack trace, the app version, your device model and operating system version, and memory/battery state at the time of the crash. It does not contain your IP address, device name, name, or any of the content you have entered into the app. We do not use Sentry for analytics, performance tracing, or session recording, and crash reports are never used for advertising. Reports are deleted automatically after 90 days. See Sentry's Privacy Policy.

Expo / React Native infrastructure No analytics or usage telemetry is included in this build. Expo Go itself may collect usage data when running in development; this does not apply to production builds.

2. How we use data

We do not collect, transmit, or sell personal data. All app functionality operates locally on your device. Third-party SDKs (AdMob, Sentry) operate under their own privacy policies as described above.

Crisol contains no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. We do not process payments and hold no purchase or billing data of any kind.

3. Data retention and deletion

All locally stored data can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the app. We have no access to your device data and cannot assist with remote deletion.

Crash reports held by Sentry are deleted automatically after 90 days. To have them deleted sooner, contact us (section 6) with the approximate date and time of the crash and your device model; we cannot otherwise link a report to you.

4. Children's privacy

Crisol is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through third-party SDKs, contact those providers directly.

5. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the policy changes and note the new effective date above.
