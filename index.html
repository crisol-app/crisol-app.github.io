What it does

Five trackers that normally cost five subscriptions, in one interface that behaves like a tool instead of a dashboard.

  • Quests

    Daily habits, side quests and main quests, with sub-tasks and deadlines. Finish one and it pays XP. Streaks come from what you actually completed.

  • Diet & macros

    Log meals by calories, protein, carbs and fat. Search a bundled food table and Open Food Facts; results cache on your device, so logging works with no signal.

  • Hydration

    Track water against a daily target, with reminders that fire on your schedule rather than someone else's idea of a wellness nudge.

  • Workouts

    Build routines from an exercise library with sets, reps and muscle groups. A rest and interval timer and a workout clock are built in.

  • Notes

    Freeform notes, checklists, folders and tags. No forced structure, no templates you have to fight.

  • Calendar

    Events with dates, times and reminders, sitting in the same planner as the quests they belong to.

  • Progress

    XP, levels and streaks derived from your completion history. No loot boxes, no pay-to-win, no confetti — just the numbers.

  • Personal targets

    Calorie and macro goals calculated from your body data and activity level (Mifflin-St Jeor and AMDR), in metric or imperial.

Your data

Crisol is local-first because there is nowhere else for your data to go. We do not run a server that holds it.

  • It lives on your device. Quests, meals, notes, workouts and body measurements are stored in a local database and never sent to us.
  • No account. No sign-up, no email, no password. Uninstalling the app deletes everything.
  • Ads keep it free. A Google AdMob banner funds the app, and we request non-personalized ads by default.
  • Crash reports only. If the app crashes, Sentry receives the error and your device model — never your content. Reports are deleted after 90 days.
  • Food search is the one network call. Searching sends the term you typed to Open Food Facts, with no identifier attached.
